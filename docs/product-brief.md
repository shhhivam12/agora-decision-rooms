# Agora Decision Rooms product brief

**Talk together. Decide together.**

Agora Decision Rooms helps a group turn a conversation into one shared plan. Kabir serves the room, while the Smart Stage shows preferences, planning checks, options, member votes and host confirmation.

## First journey: friends planning an outing

Create a room and invite up to four members by code. Talk in English, Hindi or Hinglish; collect budget, diet, indoor preferences and meeting details. Discover real venues and check listed reservation policy/hours, weather and driving estimates. Compare source-linked results, change the brief and vote from each person's own device. The host confirms only after every current member supports the same plan.

## What makes the journey useful

- One visible workspace beside voice, people and captions.
- Agentic public reads with sources, check times, missing-context follow-ups and recovery.
- Each person's preferences remain attributed and editable.
- Consent belongs to people: authenticated votes and revision-aware confirmation.
- A microphone-free entry supports actual shared planning through typing.
- English/Hindi interfaces and Kabir's audio-reactive avatar keep the experience approachable.

## Current outcome and scope

The outcome is a confirmed shared plan with votes and host approval. The app does not book, pay or write a calendar event. Venue prices, indoor seating and exact table availability stay unknown when the public source does not provide them. Driving estimates exclude live traffic.

The full shared room is a browser experience for laptop and Android Chrome with FastAPI running. The native test APK includes the refreshed UI, guided outing and native Agora voice entry. GitHub Pages is a static guided preview. The offline guided journey uses labelled sample venues and simulated votes.

Persistent accounts, private constraints, external calendar writes, booking and payments are future integrations. The existing cast represents people visually; there is one AI facilitator per live room.
